# 1:1 prep: Staff growth and ownership

## Goal

Leave the conversation with a concrete Staff-level ownership bet, an explicit bar, named gaps, and a timeline for reviewing progress.

## Opener

> I want to be deliberate about earning Staff, not vague about it. I’m looking for a concrete ownership bet, a clear bar, and direct feedback on the gaps I need to close. Can we leave this conversation with those written down?

## Questions

1. What would you need to see from me to confidently sponsor me for Staff here?
   - Ask for 3–5 observable behaviors and examples from people already operating at that level.

2. Which should become my Staff-shaped ownership bet: Connectors or AI Studio?
   - Ask which has the business importance, cross-functional surface area, and durability to demonstrate Staff impact.

3. Can we define the outcome, boundaries, and decision rights for that bet?
   - What problem must move?
   - Which teams need alignment?
   - What can I decide independently?
   - What needs escalation?
   - What is explicitly out of scope?

4. Where am I currently below the Staff bar?
   - Ask for direct gaps in technical leadership, product judgment, influence, execution through others, and communication.
   - Follow with: “What behavior would prove each gap is closed?”

5. What impact should I demonstrate beyond shipping my own frontend work?
   - Ask for a named opportunity and measure. Don’t accept “be more strategic” without either.

6. Who needs to see this work to support a promotion case?
   - Manager, product, design, backend/platform, and adjacent engineering leads.
   - Ask for help creating the right exposure.

7. What is the promotion timeline and decision process?
   - Next calibration window.
   - Evidence expected.
   - Who writes or sponsors the packet.
   - When to expect a clear readiness read.

8. Can we make this a recurring checkpoint?
   - Every 2–4 weeks: evidence, missing proof, and course correction.

## Evidence to bring

The August 18 1:1 assigned Connectors cleanup and AI Studio rework, but left priority, scope, definition of done, and timeline unstated. Use that ambiguity as the first manager-level problem to resolve.
